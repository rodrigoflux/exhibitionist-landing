    I.

    One page out, and the whole packet is wrong.

    An evidence-heavy petition means dozens of exhibits and hundreds of pages. Your team numbers them, counts pages, stamps footers and writes a letter that cites each one. Then an exhibit splits, two merge, a section moves, and every number, page count and citation has to be fixed by hand.

    “Exhibitionist began on exactly that failure: one page removed after the table was printed.”

    Now numbers, page counts, citations and exports all come from one record.

    One removed page, by hand
    1. 1Table of exhibits exported and proofread
    2. 2One duplicate page removed from Exhibit I.A.2
    3. 3Every later exhibit now starts one page earlier
    4. 4Printed table no longer matches the packet
    5. 5"See Exh. p.212" in the letter now points to the wrong page
